# Today Film Makers Website Copy and Design Direction

## Website Goal

Create a premium brand-partnership website for Today Film Makers.

The experience should feel cinematic, editorial and highly intentional. It should use Apple-style restraint in the hero, then move into more expressive Awwwards-inspired motion systems as the visitor explores the page.

The website must never present unverified campaign data, demo labels or placeholder proof.

## Visual Rules

- Primary background: deep black
- Accent: cinematic yellow
- Supporting surface: warm off-white
- Typography: oversized modern sans serif paired with a refined editorial serif
- Motion: slow, controlled and cinematic
- Images: no stock image in the hero or Campaign Proof
- Data: no star markers, footnote markers or hidden disclaimers beside metrics
- Views period: always label 15.5M+ as a 90-day figure, never monthly
- Audience geography: do not highlight India
- Mobile: every motion composition must remain legible and usable
- Reduced motion: respect the operating system preference

## Homepage Structure

1. Text-Only Hero
2. The Audience
3. Content Formats
4. Campaign Proof
5. Why Partner With TFM
6. Collaboration Options
7. How It Works
8. Final Brand Partnership CTA
9. Footer

# 03 - Campaign Proof

## Main Headline

BUILT LIKE A FILM.  
SHAPED FOR THE FEED.

## Supporting Copy

Every campaign moves through four deliberate beats. The result is a product story that feels cinematic, useful and native to the filmmaking community.

## Creative System

### 01 - The Hook

Stop the scroll.

Open with an idea that earns the first second.

### 02 - The Product

Show the value.

Put the product inside a real filmmaker workflow.

### 03 - The Payoff

Make it useful.

Turn features into a result the audience understands.

### 04 - The Action

Move the viewer.

Finish with one clear reason to explore the brand.

## Motion Design Direction

- Do not use video
- Do not use photography
- Build a pure motion-design graphic inspired by filmmaking tools
- Use a cinematic focus reticle
- Use animated framing corners
- Use timecode and live-composition labels
- Use four changing story frames
- Use an edit timeline with four clips
- Use a moving playhead
- Use a responsive audio waveform
- Allow the visitor to select any of the four campaign beats
- Keep all numbers conceptual to the four-step story system, not fake campaign performance
